About us

Built for developers
who love to ship.

Autocode AI started as a simple idea: what if your coding assistant lived right next to your editor, could read photos of code, and could run what you write — without jumping between tabs?

Our mission

Make AI pair-programming feel native. We combine a real code editor, Gemini-powered assist, OpenCV + OCR for scanned code, and a safe online runner so you stay in flow from first line to working output.

What we believe

Tools should get out of the way. Every button — Explain, Fix, Optimize, Complete, Run, Scan — is designed to answer a real moment in a developer’s day, with tight answers and clean code blocks.

The stack

Flask on the backend, CodeMirror in the browser, Google Gemini for language understanding, OpenCV + Tesseract for vision, and Razorpay for simple subscriptions when you need more capacity.
